# Leave categories

Rules for each leave category, from the 15 October 2026 functional specification (leave management, PDF pages 1–17) and the confirmed decisions in `LEAVE_POLICY.md`. Claims are excluded. HR approves or rejects every category; nobody may decide their own request.

| Leave category | Rules | Open questions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Annual | Full-time base 14/14/15/18 days by level plus 0–3 service days through under eight completed years; January–December balance; joining month counted in full; cumulative earned amount rounded up once to a half day; only earnings to date may be used; uniform 4/8-hour Monday–Friday dates; approval debit and future-cancellation reversal in one transaction. | Last-year proration, eight-plus-year reward, part-time/intern policy and 29 February anniversary are on hold. Public holidays are not automatically excluded. New planned requests are allowed after 15 December. |
| Annual carry-forward | See `carry-forward-and-calendar.md`. | Expiry and forfeiture postings stay disabled pending policy review. |
| Medical / Sick | Full-time calendar-year 14/18/22-day grant by service; submitted in the app within 48 hours, measured from midnight Malaysia time on the start date; a saved certificate is required before approval; 4/8-hour weekdays; debit and future-cancellation reversal. | A saved file does not prove the certificate is valid; HR must inspect it. Part-time/intern coverage is unresolved; do not assign zero. |
| Hospitalisation | Separate 60-day calendar-year grant; saved medical evidence required; 4/8-hour weekdays; debit and reversal. The company's combined 60-day figure (Medical plus Hospitalisation use) is shown separately for reporting. | HR must check the evidence. The combined company figure must never be the only approval limit. |
| Marriage | First legal marriage declaration, official registration date, three consecutive full calendar days after registration and within 12 months, completed probation, uploaded certificate, and no earlier approved Marriage Leave. Approval checks every saved full-day date. Approved future cancellation removes the shared-calendar absence without a balance reversal. | HR must review the certificate and declaration. |
| Maternity | 98 consecutive full calendar days, End Date derived from Start Date, expected confinement date and formal notice date captured. No separate HR-verification stamp is needed before approval. Approval checks every saved full-day date. Approved future cancellation removes the shared-calendar absence without a balance reversal. | Eligibility, allowance circumstances and notice exceptions need HR judgement; do not reject on a rigid 60-day notice formula. |
| Paternity | Seven consecutive full calendar days from the actual birth date, End Date derived, notice and expected/actual birth dates captured, at least 12 months' service, duplicate-birth prevention and a five-confinement limit. Approval checks every saved full-day date. Approved future cancellation removes the shared-calendar absence without a balance reversal. | Marriage, evidence and 30-day notice circumstances are not machine-checked; HR must assess them. |
| Compassionate | One of the specified close relationships, three consecutive full calendar days with exact saved date rows, and an uploaded supporting document before approval. Future approved cancellation removes the shared-calendar absence without a balance reversal. | HR must verify the document and relationship. |
| Emergency | Request and evidence may be submitted after the leave. Approval requires evidence, exact saved weekday rows and an Annual Leave debit in the same transaction, spending carry-forward first where applicable. HR may correct an approved past Emergency Leave with a reason, restoring the exact Annual/carry-forward split once; the employee cannot use that exception. | Ordinary Annual Leave request timing is unchanged. |
| Training | A training category and exact saved weekday entries are required. Company-required training has no debit; employee-requested external training charges earned Annual Leave, carry-forward first, with exact future-cancellation reversal. | — |
| Unpaid | Employees may choose it without exhausting paid leave. A written reason, exact saved weekdays and an HR decision are required; approval has no paid-balance debit and cancellation has no reversal. | — |
| Replacement | Deferred at the company's request. | Keep unavailable for approval; do not infer public-holiday or earning rules. |
| Off in lieu | Generic weekday range only. | HR has not set the earning amount or expiry for approved extra work. Keep the type unavailable and approval blocked; do not assume a credit rate. |

## Cross-cutting rules

- A category's generic date rule is not evidence that its eligibility, evidence and accounting are complete. Reject approval for categories whose rules are not implemented, rather than approving without accounting.
- Approved-future-leave cancellation covers Annual, Sick, Hospitalisation, Marriage, Maternity, Paternity, Compassionate and Emergency. Categories that use no balance remove only the shared-calendar absence. Corrections after a closing year's carry-forward confirmation are blocked until a reconciliation process is designed.
- The company chose manual leave-date selection over automatic public-holiday exclusion. Do not implement the specification's holiday-exclusion language without a new company decision.
- The specification's HR acknowledgement step is superseded: approval and cancellation take effect on the decision, and HR is notified by email.
- Decision and cancellation emails apply to every active leave type, not only Annual.
