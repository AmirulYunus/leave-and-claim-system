## Cross-cutting findings

- The main employee form generates its date rows in the request transaction. A category's generic date rule is not evidence that its eligibility, evidence and accounting are complete.
- A read-only Developer-environment Leave Type inspection on 26 October found Annual, Sick, Hospitalisation, Marriage, Maternity, Paternity, Compassionate, Emergency, Training and Unpaid active. Replacement and Off in lieu remain unavailable. The server rejects approval for unsupported categories instead of silently approving without accounting.
- The deployed approved-future-leave cancellation server permits Marriage, Maternity, Paternity, Compassionate and Emergency in addition to Annual, Sick and Hospitalisation. The Emergency path restores the saved Annual/carry-forward split for future cancellation and a reasoned HR past-date correction. Corrections after a closing year's carry-forward confirmation are held by the ledger guard until a separate reconciliation process is designed.
- The company explicitly chose manual leave-date selection rather than automated public-holiday exclusion. The PDF's holiday-exclusion language must not be implemented without a new company decision.
- The PDF's HR acknowledgement step is superseded by the user's decision: ordinary approval and cancellation take effect on the authorized decision, with HR notified. HR may decide regardless of Superior absence, and self-approval is forbidden.
- Notification and shared-calendar behavior has been demonstrated for Annual; other enabled categories still need genuine end-to-end checks for employee/HR email, calendar display and cancellation cleanup.
- The active decision-email flows are driven by status changes on Leave Request and Leave Cancellation, without a leave-type filter. This supports all active types structurally, but delivery still needs genuine end-to-end verification for the other categories.
- LR-0066 and LR-0067 were inspected read-only in the Superior app on 25 October 2026. Their missing category fields and date ranges explain why approval should be blocked under the current rules. Neither request was edited or approved. Do not create fictional leave requests for testing.

## Recommended next work

1. Have the request owner correct LR-0066 or LR-0067 only if the underlying request is genuine and eligible, then test the resulting approval with the authorized Superior. The currently saved records cannot establish a successful Maternity/Paternity approval path.
2. Verify an eligible approved-future Marriage, Maternity or Paternity cancellation in a genuine employee account. These categories do not consume the Annual/Sick/Hospitalisation ledger, so their cancellation must remove the shared-calendar absence without a balance reversal.
3. Continue allowing planned Annual Leave requests after 15 December as directed by the user. Keep the separate written higher-limit deadline and manual leave-date selection; no automated public-holiday calendar is required by the confirmed company policy.
4. Complete the remaining leave types one category at a time, pairing each form with evidence, server eligibility, decision authority, accounting and cancellation before enabling it for employees.
