Automatic earned-leave grants

The daily grant flow credits only the increase in cumulative leave earned through completed calendar months. Save HR employment details for an employee who has no balance period. Older development test records may still contain excess historical credit; the approval check does not allow unearned leave to be used.

Hospitalisation allowance

For a full-time employee, grant the current calendar year's Hospitalisation allowance before approving a Hospitalisation request. Repeating the grant adds no duplicate credit. The company combined figure shown on the Employee form also subtracts approved Medical Leave; Hospitalisation approval retains its separate statutory 60-day minimum.
